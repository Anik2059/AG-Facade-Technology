INDUSTRY INSIGHTS

Why facade engineering matters in Bangladesh today

Public reporting on Dhaka's construction market shows what clients and architects are asking for, and where quality makes the difference.

01

The glass skyline is growing

Dhaka has seen a surge in glass-clad buildings, especially corporate towers in areas such as Tejgaon, Banani and Gulshan. Architects favour double-glazed Low-E glass for the strong tropical sun.

02

Heat is a real design issue

Experts warn that extensive glass facades can intensify urban heat. One study cited in the press found Dhaka's urban heat island intensity rising by about 0.21°C per decade since 1995.

03

Performance is up to the specifier

Reports note that the publicly available BNBC 2015 and 2020 lack detailed mandatory glazing requirements such as U-value and solar heat gain. Choosing thermal-break frames and Low-E glazing voluntarily sets a higher standard.

04

Aluminium is replacing timber

Aluminium extrusion for doors, windows, railings and curtain walls has been growing as timber gets scarcer. Builders value it for being light, strong, corrosion-resistant and easy to maintain.

Sources: The Business Standard, UNB, The Daily Star (via Foundry Planet). TBS · UNB · Daily Star
